# Jose Vigil
### AI Systems Architecture, Grounded in Continuity Research

---

## The Problem

Most AI tooling today is episodic. Each session starts over. Context, intent
and institutional knowledge get lost between tools, sessions and providers.

Teams pay for this twice: once in token costs re-establishing context that
should have persisted, and again in judgment quality when AI systems drift
from what people actually meant.

---

## What I Bring

I design and build AI-assisted systems for companies, informed by original
research into cognitive continuity — the problem most AI vendors still treat
as a UX detail rather than an architecture question (see Cognituum, my
ongoing research initiative).

In practice, that means:

- **Systems architecture for AI-assisted workflows** that preserve context
  and intent across sessions, tools and team members
- **Reducing token waste and rework** caused by context loss between sessions
- **Provider-agnostic design** — avoiding lock-in to a single AI vendor.
  *In plain terms: your team isn't stuck re-negotiating or rebuilding from
  scratch if you switch AI providers.*
- **Local knowledge architectures** that evolve with your team's actual use,
  instead of static prompt libraries that go stale. *In plain terms: less
  time and budget spent constantly rewriting prompts as your team and
  product change.*

---

## The Philosophy Behind the Work — C.O.M.

**Creativity Originates in Mankind.**

AI executes, amplifies and accelerates. The judgment, intention and meaning
your systems need to protect are yours. I build accordingly: systems
designed around your team's continuity and decision-making, not around
whatever is easiest to automate.

## Why Now

AI adoption inside companies is accelerating faster than the infrastructure
to support it responsibly. Most implementations optimize for a single
session or a single tool. Few are built to survive a provider switch, a team
handoff, or six months of accumulated context.

That gap is where the work happens.
