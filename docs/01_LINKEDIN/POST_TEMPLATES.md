# POST TEMPLATES

> Reusable structures for LinkedIn content. Adapt voice and specifics;
> preserve structure.

---

## Template 1 — Contrarian Observation (Pillar 1)

```
[Observation most people accept without questioning]

But here's what I keep noticing:

[Counter-observation from real experience or research]

[One implication of that observation]

[C.O.M. or cognitive continuity angle]

This is what I'm building toward with [Bloomaut / Cognituum].
```

---

## Template 2 — Research Note (Pillar 2)

```
Working on Cognituum this week, I ran into something worth sharing:

[Specific technical or conceptual problem encountered]

The standard solution is [X]. But that breaks down when [specific condition].

What I'm testing instead: [Brief description of approach]

Early result: [Honest assessment]

[Open question for the audience]
```

---

## Template 3 — Founder Reflection (Pillar 3)

```
[Year / moment] — I made a decision that cost me [X].

At the time I thought [mistaken belief].

What I understand now: [Reframe]

The pattern I see in hindsight: [Generalizable insight]

If you're building [relevant context], this is worth considering.
```

---

## Template 4 — Product Signal (Pillar 4)

```
Something I built into Bloomaut that most content platforms miss:

[Specific feature or design decision]

Why it matters: [Connection to human creativity / community / validation]

The assumption I'm challenging: [Dominant paradigm being questioned]

[Call to conversation or early access]
```

---

## Rules for all posts

- No more than 5 lines before the first line break
- End with a question or a clear point — never a vague call to action
- Never use "I'm excited to announce"
- Never use "Game changer" or "Revolutionary"
- One idea per post
