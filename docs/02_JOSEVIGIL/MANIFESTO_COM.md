# MANIFESTO — C.O.M.

## Creativity Originates in Mankind

*A design philosophy for human-centric systems.*

---

## The Claim

Artificial intelligence can execute.
Systems can scale.
Automation can accelerate.

But the origin of creative intention —
the question of *why* something should be made,
*what* it should mean,
and *who* it is for —
that originates in human beings.

This is not a romantic claim.
It is an architectural one.

---

## Why This Matters Now

The current AI conversation is dominated by two positions:

1. AI will replace human creativity.
2. AI is just a tool; humans remain fully in control.

Both positions miss what is actually happening:

AI is changing the *infrastructure* of creation
without changing its *origin*.

The implication is significant:

Systems designed to replace human creativity will fail at meaning.
Systems designed to extend human creativity will succeed at scale.

---

## The Design Principle

Build systems that:

- Preserve human intention across workflow transitions
- Amplify creative output without erasing its origin
- Maintain cognitive continuity across AI interactions
- Serve human judgment, not replace it
