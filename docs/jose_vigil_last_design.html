Two nodes are currently running, plus this hub itself — where research, essays and the technical record live. Each is a distinct system with its own architecture, purpose and audience — connected by a shared philosophy of building things that persist.

SYS::01
Cognituum
Active Research
A Cognitive State Continuum Infrastructure

Most AI tooling today is episodic — each session starts over, and context, intent and institutional knowledge get lost between tools, sessions and providers. Cognituum is my ongoing research initiative into cognitive continuity: how systems can preserve context and intent instead of losing them. Output is a whitepaper / framework specification, in progress, intended as a foundation for future standards, protocols or product implementations.
