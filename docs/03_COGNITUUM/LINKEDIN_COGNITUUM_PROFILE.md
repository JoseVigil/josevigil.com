## About Section

```
Cognituum is a research initiative building the infrastructure so your
engineering decisions stay decided — instead of being re-litigated every
time you open a new AI session or switch providers.

Right now, every AI-assisted session starts a negotiation you already won
last week. You explain the architecture, the constraints, the reasoning —
and the model forgets, session after session. That costs tokens spent
re-establishing context that should have simply persisted, and judgment
quality lost when a model quietly overrides a call that should have
stayed yours.

Cognituum is built on a simple inversion: your engineering decisions
outrank the model's. Once you decide something, it's captured and
enforced, not re-negotiated tool by tool. It's provider-agnostic by
design — Claude, Codex, Gemini, whatever you use, your judgment sits
above all of them.

We're in active development, opening early access to a small group of
senior engineers and developers before public availability.
```
