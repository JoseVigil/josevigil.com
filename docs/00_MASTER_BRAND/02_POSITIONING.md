# POSITIONING

## Core Position

Jose Vigil occupies the intersection of:

- Human creativity advocacy in an AI-dominated conversation
- Practical systems architecture built on that philosophy
- Research into cognitive continuity and AI infrastructure

---

## What Makes This Position Distinctive

Most AI narratives argue for one of two extremes:

1. AI will replace human creativity entirely.
2. AI is just a tool; humans remain fully in control.

Jose Vigil argues a third position:

> AI changes the architecture of creativity —
> but the origin, the intention and the meaning
> remain irreducibly human.

This is C.O.M.
