# BIO — MEDIUM (1 paragraph)

## Active Version

Jose Vigil has spent over 25 years at the intersection of design, engineering
and entrepreneurship — building software products, leading technical teams and
founding startups across content, automation and AI infrastructure.

His foundational philosophy — C.O.M., Creativity Originates in Mankind —
argues that the most durable AI systems will be those that preserve and
amplify human continuity rather than replace it.

He is the founder of Bloomaut, a community platform for human-validated
content creation, and Cognituum, a research initiative exploring cognitive
continuity in AI-assisted workflows.
