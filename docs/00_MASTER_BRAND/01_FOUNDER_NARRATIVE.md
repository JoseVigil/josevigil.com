# FOUNDER NARRATIVE

## One-Line Definition

> Industrial Designer turned Systems Builder researching how human creativity
> and cognitive continuity can coexist with artificial intelligence.

---

## Short Bio (2–3 sentences)

Jose Vigil is a systems builder, entrepreneur, designer and AI researcher
with more than 25 years of experience creating software products, startups,
automation systems, user experiences and technical architectures.

His work is organized around a single thesis: while AI can amplify and execute,
creativity originates in mankind — a philosophy he calls C.O.M.

He is the founder of Bloomaut and Cognituum, two projects that apply and
investigate this principle at a practical and research level.

---

## Medium Bio (paragraph)

Jose Vigil has spent over 25 years at the intersection of design, engineering
and entrepreneurship — building software products, leading technical teams and
founding multiple startups across content, automation and AI infrastructure.

Trained as an Industrial Designer and self-shaped as a systems architect,
Jose approaches technology not as an end but as a medium for extending human
intention. His foundational philosophy — C.O.M., Creativity Originates in
Mankind — argues that the most durable AI systems will be those that preserve
and amplify human continuity rather than replace it.

Today he is focused on two projects: Bloomaut, a platform for human-led
content creation validated through community feedback, and Cognituum, a
research initiative exploring cognitive continuity in AI-assisted workflows.
