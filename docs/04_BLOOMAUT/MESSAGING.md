# BLOOMAUT — MESSAGING GUIDE

## Core Message

Bloomaut gives creators and entrepreneurs a private space to validate
their content with real human feedback before publishing to the world.

---

## Message Hierarchy

### Primary
> Human creativity, validated before it reaches the world.

### Secondary
> A community platform for creators who want real feedback before they publish.

### Supporting
> AI assists the workflow. Humans validate the work.

---

## Audience-Specific Messaging

### For entrepreneurs building personal brand:
> You have ideas worth sharing. Bloomaut helps you share them
> with confidence — not by generating content for you,
> but by giving you honest feedback before the world sees it.

### For creators afraid to publish:
> The problem isn't your content. It's the lack of honest feedback
> before you hit publish. Bloomaut is that missing layer.

### For AI-skeptical professionals:
> We don't replace your creativity. We build the community
> that validates it.

---

## What to Avoid

- Positioning as "another AI content tool"
- Claiming AI generates the content (it assists the workflow)
- Overpromising virality or growth metrics
- Competing directly with publishing platforms (we're a layer before them)
