# LINKEDIN ABOUT SECTION

## Active Version

I've spent 25+ years building systems at the intersection of design,
engineering and entrepreneurship.

Trained as an Industrial Designer. Self-made as a systems architect.
Driven by a single question that keeps getting more urgent:

**What happens to human creativity when AI can execute almost anything?**

My answer is C.O.M. — *Creativity Originates in Mankind.*

A design philosophy that argues the next decade of AI won't be defined by
autonomous agents replacing people. It will be defined by systems that
preserve, amplify and extend human intention, creativity and cognitive continuity.

That thesis drives everything I build:

→ **Bloomaut** — a platform for human-led content creation validated through
community feedback before it reaches the world.

→ **Cognituum** — a research initiative exploring cognitive continuity
infrastructure: how AI systems can support continuous human cognition
rather than isolated, fragmented interactions.

I write and research on human-centric AI, system design, creative workflows
and the future of entrepreneurship in an AI-accelerated world.
