# SPEAKER INTRO

## Standard Introduction (60–90 seconds)

Our next speaker has spent over 25 years building systems at the
intersection of design, technology and entrepreneurship.

He is the founder of Bloomaut and Cognituum,
and the author of a design philosophy called C.O.M. —
Creativity Originates in Mankind.

His work explores a question that is becoming more urgent every year:

*What happens to human creativity when AI can execute almost anything?*

His answer challenges both the AI optimists and the AI skeptics —
and offers a third position that may be the most important one.

Please welcome Jose Vigil.
