# BIO — LONG (Full narrative)

## Active Version

Jose Vigil is a systems builder, entrepreneur, designer and AI researcher
with more than 25 years of experience creating software products, startups,
automation systems, user experiences and technical architectures.

Trained as an Industrial Designer, Jose spent his early career building
technical infrastructure — from helpdesk systems to software-as-a-service
products — developing a deep understanding of how systems fail and how
they can be made to persist.

Over two decades of founding and leading startups, Jose developed a
perspective that runs counter to the dominant AI narrative:

While artificial intelligence can amplify, accelerate and execute —
genuine creativity, intuition, judgment, meaning and purpose
originate in human beings.

He calls this C.O.M.: Creativity Originates in Mankind.
A design philosophy for human-centric systems.

This thesis drives his two current projects:

**Bloomaut** is a platform and community that helps entrepreneurs and
creators generate, validate and refine content through human peer feedback
and AI-assisted workflows. It is the commercial, applied layer of the C.O.M.
philosophy.

**Cognituum** is a research initiative exploring cognitive continuity
infrastructure — how AI systems can support continuous human cognition
rather than isolated, fragmented interactions. It represents the
research and architectural foundation of the ecosystem.

Jose writes and speaks on human-centric AI, system design, entrepreneurship
and the future of creative work in an AI-accelerated world.

He is based in Argentina and works internationally.
