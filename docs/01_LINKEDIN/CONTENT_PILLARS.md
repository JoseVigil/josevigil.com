# CONTENT PILLARS

> All LinkedIn content (posts, articles, reposts, comments) must map
> to one of these four pillars. If a piece of content doesn't fit,
> it doesn't get published.

---

## Pillar 1 — Human-Centric AI (C.O.M.)

**Core question:** What is irreducibly human in a world of AI execution?

Topics:
- The limits of AI creativity
- Where human judgment cannot be automated
- C.O.M. philosophy applied to real examples
- Token economics and the cost of AI dependence
- AI narratives worth questioning

Tone: Analytical, confident, contrarian when warranted.

---

## Pillar 2 — Cognitive Continuity (Cognituum)

**Core question:** How do we preserve context, intention and continuity
across AI-assisted workflows?

Topics:
- Context fragmentation in current AI tools
- Local knowledge architectures
- Intent orchestration models
- Research observations and hypotheses
- The gap between AI memory and human continuity

Tone: Research-driven, technical, forward-looking.

---

## Pillar 3 — Entrepreneurship Lessons

**Core question:** What does 25 years of building actually teach you?

Topics:
- Systems thinking in startups
- When to pivot vs when to persist
- Building products in isolation vs validation
- The difference between vision and market fit
- Honest reflections on what worked and what didn't

Tone: Direct, honest, experience-grounded. No failure porn.

---

## Pillar 4 — Creative Systems (Bloomaut)

**Core question:** How do creators validate and improve content before
it reaches the world?

Topics:
- The problem with AI-generated video without human review
- Community feedback as a creative infrastructure
- Workflow design for content creators
- The difference between automated content and human-originated content

Tone: Practical, community-oriented, founder perspective.

---

## Distribution Guide

| Format | Recommended pillar |
|--------|-------------------|
| Short posts (text only) | Pillars 1 & 3 |
| Long-form articles | Pillars 1 & 2 |
| Behind-the-scenes | Pillar 4 |
| Research previews | Pillar 2 |
| Founder reflections | Pillar 3 |
