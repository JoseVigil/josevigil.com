# VISUAL IDENTITY

## Design Philosophy

Visual identity should reflect the same values as the intellectual identity:

- Precision
- Clarity
- Human intention made visible through system structure

---

## Color Direction

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Primary background | Near-black (#080808 range) |
| Primary text | Off-white (#e8e8e8 range) |
| Accent 1 | Signal green (#00ff88 range) |
| Accent 2 | Electric blue (#00b8ff range) |
| Borders/lines | Low-opacity white (6–12%) |

---

## Typography Direction

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Primary | Monospace (IBM Plex Mono / JetBrains Mono) |
| Reasoning | Reflects systems thinking; precision; technical credibility |

---

## Layout Principles

- Grid-based, structured
- Generous whitespace
- Information hierarchy: Person → Philosophy → Projects
- Never lead with product logos before the founder identity
