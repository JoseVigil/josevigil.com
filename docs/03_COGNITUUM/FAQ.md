# COGNITUUM — FAQ

## What is Cognituum?

Cognituum is a research initiative exploring how AI systems can support
continuous human cognition rather than isolated interactions.

## What problem does it solve?

Current AI tools are episodic — each session forgets the last.
Cognituum researches the infrastructure patterns that would allow
intent, context and knowledge to persist across AI interactions.

## How is this different from AI memory features in existing products?

Most "memory" features in AI products are single-vendor, opaque
and centered on conversation recall.

Cognituum is interested in architectural continuity —
how human intent flows across tools, sessions and providers
without being fragmented or lost.

## What is the output?

The primary output is a whitepaper / framework specification
that could serve as a foundation for standards, protocols
or future product implementations.

## How does this relate to Bloomaut?

Bloomaut is the commercial, applied layer.
Cognituum is the research infrastructure layer.
Both are organized around C.O.M. as the foundational philosophy.
