Research Initiative

COGNITUUM

Research · v0.4
Cognitive State Continuum for Programmable Intent

Most AI systems are stateless. Each session begins from zero. Cognituum is a framework for cognitive continuity — a protocol for machines that remember, persist and accumulate state across time.

The core thesis: AI utility is constrained not by capability but by continuity. Without persistent cognitive state, even the most capable models remain reactive tools rather than autonomous systems.
