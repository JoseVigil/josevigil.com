## Key Design Decision

The .com in JOSEVIGIL.COM is not a generic TLD.
It is the initialism of the central philosophy:

> C.O.M. — Creativity Originates in Mankind

This double reading — domain + manifesto — should be visually legible
on the homepage without being over-explained.
