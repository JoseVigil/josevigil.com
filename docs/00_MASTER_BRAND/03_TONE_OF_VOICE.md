# TONE OF VOICE

## Core Attributes

- Intelligent but accessible
- Thoughtful
- Calm
- Analytical
- Visionary
- Human-centered
- Technically credible

---

## What This Sounds Like

Writing in this voice is:

- Direct without being blunt
- Confident without being arrogant
- Technical without being inaccessible
- Philosophical without being abstract
- Honest without being self-deprecating

---

## What to Avoid

- AI hype or evangelism
- Fear-based AI narratives
- Empty productivity content
- Generic motivational tone
- Self-deprecating language or failure framing

---

## Examples

### Instead of:
> "I tried many times and failed, but I kept going."

### Write:
> "Each system I built taught me something about where intention breaks down.
> That pattern became the research."

---

### Instead of:
> "AI is going to change everything."

### Write:
> "AI changes the architecture of creation.
> It does not change its origin."

---

## Voice Checklist

Before publishing any content, verify:

- [ ] Does this reflect a point of view, not just information?
- [ ] Is it grounded in real experience or research?
- [ ] Does it avoid AI hype or fear?
- [ ] Does it reinforce C.O.M. directly or indirectly?
- [ ] Would this embarrass or strengthen the brand in 5 years?
