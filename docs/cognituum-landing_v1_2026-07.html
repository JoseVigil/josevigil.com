The Problem

Context gets lost. Teams pay for it twice.

Most AI tooling today starts over every session. Your team re-explains context, redoes work, and pays for it twice — once in token costs re-establishing what should have persisted, and again in judgment quality when the output drifts from what people actually meant.

How I Work

Cognituum — the research behind the architecture

I design and build AI-assisted systems for companies. That work is informed by Cognituum, my ongoing research into why AI systems lose context — and what architecture prevents it.

Cognituum isn't a finished product. It's the research layer behind how I build: systems designed to hold context and intent across sessions and tools, instead of just executing the next request.
