## About C.O.M.

C.O.M. — Creativity Originates in Mankind — is a design philosophy
developed by Jose Vigil that argues the next decade of AI will be
defined not by autonomous agents replacing people, but by systems
that preserve, amplify and extend human intention, creativity
and cognitive continuity.

The philosophy acts as the conceptual foundation for every project
in the Jose Vigil ecosystem.
